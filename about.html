About Me

My career has always centered around people — helping them work through challenges, develop professionally, and figure out the best way to move forward.

I bring more than 15 years of combined experience across management, human resources, leadership development, employee relations, career development, business operations, risk management, business continuity, disaster recovery, and operational resilience. Throughout my career, I’ve had the opportunity to manage, coach, mentor, teach, and develop others, from individuals just beginning their careers to experienced professionals and leaders.

One thing I’ve learned over the years is that career and workplace decisions are not always straightforward. Sometimes you know exactly where you want to go but need help getting there. Other times, you may feel stuck, overlooked, frustrated with your current situation, or simply unsure about what your next move should be. I’ve spent much of my career helping people work through those types of situations.

My experience in Human Resources and management has also allowed me to see the workplace from different perspectives. I understand the concerns employees face, but I also understand how managers and organizations approach hiring, performance, employee relations, leadership, risk, and business decisions. That experience helps me provide guidance that considers the bigger picture — not just one side of the situation.

I’ve also spent years coaching and developing others. Whether I’m helping someone prepare for an interview, strengthen their resume, work through a workplace concern, prepare for leadership, or think through a career change, I believe the best guidance starts with listening and understanding where that person is today.

That is a big part of why I created HR-RN Consulting.

Through HR-RN Consulting, I work with professionals, college students, graduating seniors, emerging leaders, and individuals who are ready for a change but may not know where to begin. I provide career and workplace strategy, resume development, mock interview preparation, career transition support, workplace navigation, leadership development, and professional advisory services.

I don't believe everyone needs the same solution. Your career, experiences, challenges, and goals are personal to you. My role is to help you sort through the noise, understand your options, recognize the value you bring, and develop practical next steps that make sense for your situation.

At the end of the day, my goal is simple: meet you where you are, help you get clear about where you want to go, and give you the guidance and strategy to confidently make your next move.

Empowering Careers. Strengthening Professionals. Developing Leaders.
